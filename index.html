FAQ

answered.

How much does Pulse cost?

Pulse Pro is $14.99/month or $79.99/year (save 55%). Every subscription starts with a 3-day free trial so you can experience everything risk-free.

How accurate is the AI coaching?

Pulse uses Google's Gemini AI to create highly personalized plans based on exercise science. The AI considers your body type, goals, experience, schedule, and equipment to create plans a trainer would charge hundreds for.

Is my data private and secure?

Absolutely. We use end-to-end encryption and never sell your data. Pulse is GDPR-compliant and gives you full control over your information.

Does Pulse work with Apple Health?

Pulse syncs with Apple Health to import steps, heart rate, sleep data, and workouts. This data helps the AI make smarter training decisions.

Can I customize my workout plan?

Yes! Swap exercises, adjust sets and reps, change workout days, or regenerate your entire plan. The AI learns from your preferences.

What makes Pulse different from other fitness apps?

Pulse is the only app that combines AI workout planning, real-time voice coaching, smart nutrition with a food scanner, social challenges, and body metrics — all in one place.
