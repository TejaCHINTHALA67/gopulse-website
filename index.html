Knowledge

Science-backed
fitness education.

Understanding the "why" behind your training is just as important as the training itself.

01

Progressive Overload

The foundation of muscle growth. Gradually increasing weight, reps, or intensity forces your body to adapt. Pulse tracks this automatically and adjusts your plan.

02

Energy Balance Science

Your body burns calories through BMR (basal metabolic rate) + exercise. Pulse calculates your exact TDEE using Mifflin-St Jeor formula and shows your deficit or surplus in real time.

03

Recovery Science

Muscles grow during rest, not during the workout. Pulse monitors 11 muscle recovery zones so you know exactly when each body part is ready for the next session.

How accurate is the AI coaching?

Pulse uses Google's Gemini AI combined with exercise science to create highly personalized plans. The AI considers your body type, goals, experience, schedule, equipment, and recovery to build plans a trainer would charge hundreds for.

Absolutely. We use end-to-end encryption and never sell your data. Pulse is privacy-first and gives you full control over your information.

Yes! Pulse syncs with Apple Health to import steps, heart rate, sleep data, and workouts. It also writes workout summaries and weight entries back to Health.
