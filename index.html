FAQ

Your questions,
answered

Everything you need to know about Pulse before getting started.

Is Pulse really free?

Yes! Pulse offers a generous free tier with AI workout plans, nutrition tracking, and community access. Premium features like advanced AI coaching and unlimited plan regeneration are available through an optional subscription.

How accurate is the AI coaching?

Pulse uses Google's Gemini AI to create highly personalized plans based on exercise science principles. The AI considers your body type, goals, experience, schedule, and available equipment to create plans that a human trainer would charge hundreds for.

Does Pulse work offline?

Core features like viewing your workout plan, logging exercises, and tracking nutrition work offline. AI coaching and social features require an internet connection. Your data syncs automatically when you're back online.

Is my data private and secure?

Absolutely. We use end-to-end encryption and never sell your data. Pulse is GDPR-compliant and gives you full control over your information, including the ability to export or delete all your data at any time.

How does Apple Health integration work?

Pulse seamlessly syncs with Apple Health to import steps, heart rate, sleep data, and workouts. This data helps the AI make smarter decisions about your training intensity, recovery, and overall wellness.

Can I customize my workout plan?

Yes! While the AI creates optimized plans, you have full control. Swap exercises, adjust sets and reps, change workout days, or regenerate your entire plan at any time. The AI learns from your preferences.

What makes Pulse different from other fitness apps?

Pulse is the only app that combines AI workout planning, real-time voice coaching, smart nutrition tracking with a food scanner, social challenges, and body metrics — all in one place. Stop paying for 5 separate apps.
