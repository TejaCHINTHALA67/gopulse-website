How much does Pulse cost?

Pulse Pro is $14.99/month or $79.99/year (save 55%). Every subscription starts with a 3-day free trial so you can experience everything risk-free.

How accurate is the AI coaching?

Pulse uses Google's Gemini AI combined with exercise science. The AI considers your body type, goals, experience, schedule, equipment, and recovery to build plans a trainer would charge hundreds for.

Is my data private and secure?

Absolutely. We use end-to-end encryption and never sell your data. Pulse is privacy-first and gives you full control over your information.

Does Pulse work with Apple Health?

Yes! Pulse syncs with Apple Health to import steps, heart rate, sleep, and workouts. It also writes workout summaries and weight entries back to Health.

What makes Pulse different?

Pulse is the only app that combines AI workout planning, real-time voice coaching, smart nutrition with a food scanner, energy balance tracking, muscle recovery heatmap, and gamification — all in one place.

Will Pulse be available on Android?

Yes! Pulse is built cross-platform. Launching first on iOS, with Android following shortly after. Pre-register now to be first in line.
